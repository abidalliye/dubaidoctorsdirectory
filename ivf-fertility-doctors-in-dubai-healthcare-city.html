Patient Navigation Guide for Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC)

Established in 2002 by His Highness Sheikh Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC) is the world's first medical free zone community. For patients seeking fertility care, DHCC houses the highest concentration of specialized cleanroom embryology laboratories, clinical andrologists, and reproductive endocrinologists in the United Arab Emirates.

Ibn Sina Medical Complex (Bldg 27)

Home to Orchid Fertility Clinic, First Genomix, and advanced diagnostic suites with multi-level covered patient parking.

Al Razi Medical Complex (Bldg 64)

Houses outpatient specialist consultation suites, First IVF Center, and specialized gynecological ultrasound facilities.

Transport & Metro Access

Conveniently accessible via Dubai Metro Green Line (Dubai Healthcare City Station) with direct pedestrian bridges to clinics.
