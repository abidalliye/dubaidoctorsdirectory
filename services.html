Frequently Asked Questions on DHA Services

Essential information regarding licensing, procedure pricing, and insurance coverage.

How are clinical healthcare services regulated by the DHA in Dubai?

The Dubai Health Authority enforces strict licensing standards through the Health Regulation Sector (HRS) and the Ejada quality framework, inspecting medical equipment, clinical room specifications, and professional qualifications.

Are all 30 healthcare services covered under mandatory Dubai health insurance?

Medically indicated diagnostic evaluations, outpatient consultations, urgent medical care, and inpatient surgeries are covered under DHA-mandated insurance. Specialized fertility procedures and cosmetic surgeries are covered under enhanced policies or executive riders.

How can I verify a specialist's DHA medical license in Dubai?

You can verify any physician's active license and clinical title (General Practitioner, Specialist, or Consultant) using the official DHA Sheryan portal by entering the doctor's name or DHA license ID.

Can I request at-home medical visits or home laboratory blood draws for these services?

Yes, DHA-licensed home healthcare companies provide doctor home visits, skilled nursing, and certified mobile phlebotomists for convenient at-home medical care across all Dubai neighborhoods.
