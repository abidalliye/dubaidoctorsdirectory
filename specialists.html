Frequently Asked Questions on Dubai Medical Specialists

What is the difference between a Specialist and a Consultant in Dubai?

Under DHA PQR rules, a Consultant has completed advanced fellowship training with at least 5-8 years of post-specialization clinical experience and holds senior supervisory privileges.

Are specialist consultations covered by basic Dubai health insurance?

Yes, with a referral from a General Practitioner, specialist consultations are covered under standard network plans with standard co-pay.
