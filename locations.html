Frequently Asked Questions on Dubai Healthcare Districts

Which medical district has the most specialists in Dubai?

Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC) has the highest concentration of specialized clinics with over 160 clinical facilities and 1,200 licensed physicians.

Are clinics in Dubai residential areas open late?

Yes, polyclinics in residential districts like Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, and Al Barsha frequently offer evening consultations up to 9:00 PM.
