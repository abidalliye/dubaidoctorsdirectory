Frequently Asked Questions About Healthcare in Dubai Healthcare City

Essential clinical information, licensing verification, insurance coverage, and transit details.

What makes Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC) the premier medical hub in Dubai?

DHCC is the world's largest specialized healthcare freezone, established by royal decree and regulated by the Dubai Healthcare City Authority (DHCR) in coordination with DHA. It brings together over 160 accredited clinical facilities, teaching hospitals, and internationally recognized fertility institutes within a walkable, centralized medical district.

How do I verify if a fertility doctor in DHCC holds an active license?

All doctors featured on FertiFind Dubai carry verified DHCR/DHA professional licenses. You can verify any specialist's license number through the official Dubai Health Authority registry (Sheryan portal) or the DHCR professional register, ensuring active board certification and clinical privileges.

What are the standard consultation fees for fertility specialists in DHCC?

Specialist consultation tariffs in DHCC typically range between AED 500 and AED 750 for an initial 45-to-60 minute evaluation with an internationally board-certified consultant. Subsequent follow-up visits generally range from AED 350 to AED 500, aligning with DHA tariff benchmarks.

Do clinics in DHCC accept UAE health insurance for fertility appointments?

Yes. While elective IVF cycles require specialized riders or out-of-pocket payment, primary diagnostic workups—including hormonal blood panels, ultrasound scans, hysteroscopy, and underlying endocrine treatments—are widely covered under major UAE insurance networks such as Saada, Enaya, NextCare, MetLife, Cigna, AXA, and Daman.

Can diagnostic labs in DHCC collect blood and semen samples from home?

Yes. Multiple premier diagnostic centers located in DHCC (including PureHealth and Al Borg) provide licensed phlebotomist house calls across all Dubai districts for routine blood collection, AMH hormone testing, prenatal screenings, and temperature-controlled sample transit.

What metro transit and parking facilities are available in DHCC?

Dubai Healthcare City has its own dedicated Metro Station on the Green Line, offering direct covered pedestrian access to clinical buildings 27, 33, 47, and 64 (Al Razi Complex). In addition, all major medical buildings feature dedicated valet parking services and multi-story RTA parking structures.

Are telemedicine and remote video consultations available for doctors in DHCC?

Yes. Most senior consultants in DHCC offer DHA-compliant telehealth video consultations for initial case reviews, second opinions on previous IVF cycles, and review of diagnostic ultrasound or lab findings before in-person procedures.

Can international patients travel to DHCC for medical tourism treatment?

Dubai Healthcare City is the flagship hub for Dubai Health Experience (DXH). Clinics provide fast-track medical tourism visas, dedicated international patient liaisons, translation services, airport transfers, and bundled treatment packages for overseas patients.
