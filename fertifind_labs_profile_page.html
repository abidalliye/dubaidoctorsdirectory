About Igenomix Dubai

Igenomix Dubai is a global pioneer in reproductive genetics, providing evidence-based molecular diagnostics to fertility clinics, hospitals, and couples across the UAE and GCC. Located in Dubai Healthcare City, the state-of-the-art facility is licensed by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA) and accredited to international ISO 15189 and CAP standards.

Specializing in Pre-implantation Genetic Testing for Aneuploidies (PGT-A), Endometrial Receptivity Analysis (ERA), Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing (NIPT), and comprehensive male and female reproductive hormone panels, Igenomix helps optimize embryo selection and maximize IVF success rates.
