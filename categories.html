Frequently Asked Questions on Dubai Healthcare Facilities

How are hospitals categorized under the DHA Role Delineation Framework?

DHA classifies clinical facilities from RDL 1 (Primary Clinic) to RDL 6 (Advanced Research & Teaching Hospital) based on ICU capabilities, subspecialty surgical suites, and diagnostic imaging equipment.

Are diagnostic laboratories in Dubai accredited internationally?

Yes, licensed laboratories participate in the College of American Pathologists (CAP) and ISO-15189 accreditation programs to ensure diagnostic precision.
