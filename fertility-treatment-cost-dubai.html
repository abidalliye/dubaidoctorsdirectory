🇦🇪 Dubai Health Authority & Insurance Coverage Notice

In the UAE, standard private health insurance policies typically cover diagnostic investigations, baseline consultations, pelvic ultrasounds, and diagnostic hysteroscopy. However, active IVF laboratory procedures (such as ICSI, blastocyst culture, and medication injections) are generally classified as out-of-pocket self-pay unless your employer provides an executive fertility rider. Always verify exact policy inclusions with your insurance desk.
