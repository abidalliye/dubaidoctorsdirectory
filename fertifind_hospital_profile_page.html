About American Hospital Dubai IVF Center

Established in 1996, American Hospital Dubai is a premier multi-specialty medical center accredited by the Joint Commission International (JCI) and the College of American Pathologists (CAP). Its Center for Reproductive Medicine combines leading reproductive endocrinologists, cutting-edge embryology laboratories, and full-spectrum inpatient maternal-fetal care.

In partnership with specialized clinics including Orchid Fertility Clinic in Dubai Healthcare City, the center provides comprehensive assisted reproductive technology (ART), personalized ovarian stimulation protocols, advanced pre-implantation genetic screening (PGT-A/M), and male infertility interventions.

Clinical Treatments & Fertility Services

🧬
In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) & ICSI

Custom stimulation protocols, egg retrieval in accredited surgical theaters, ICSI micro-fertilization, blastocyst culture, and embryo transfer.

❄️
Egg, Sperm & Embryo Cryopreservation

Advanced vitrification protocols for medical and social fertility preservation with long-term nitrogen tank storage compliant with UAE regulations.

🔬
Pre-implantation Genetic Testing (PGT-A & PGT-M)

Comprehensive chromosome screening and monogenic single-gene defect prevention conducted in partnership with Igenomix Dubai genetics lab.

⚕️
Male Infertility & Surgical Sperm Retrieval

PESA, TESA, and Micro-TESE surgical procedures performed by certified urologists for obstructive and non-obstructive azoospermia.

🏥
Full Inpatient Maternity, Delivery & Level III NICU

Immediate on-site pediatric support and specialized maternal-fetal medicine for high-risk twin and triplet pregnancies resulting from fertility treatment.
