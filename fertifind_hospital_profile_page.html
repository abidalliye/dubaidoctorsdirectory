About CityCare Women's Hospital

CityCare Women's Hospital is an accredited multispecialty hospital in Dubai Healthcare City designed around women's health, obstetrics, reproductive endocrinology, and child healthcare. The hospital combines inpatient surgical suites, an advanced neonatal intensive care unit (NICU), and comprehensive diagnostic laboratories to support patients through every stage of their healthcare journey.

Hospital History & Milestones

Established in Dubai Healthcare City, CityCare began as a dedicated women's health center and has grown into a major tertiary hospital. The facility is fully licensed by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA) and holds international gold accreditation from the Joint Commission International (JCI).

When Should You Visit a Specialist?

A clinical decision guide to help you find the appropriate department for your symptoms or health milestones.

🌸
Women's Health

Schedule gynecology visits for persistent pelvic pain, abnormal bleeding, hormone imbalances, or annual cervical screenings.

🧬
Fertility Concerns

Seek a specialist evaluation if conception has not occurred after 12 months of trying (or 6 months if female partner is over 35).

🤰
Pregnancy & Maternity

Contact obstetrics upon confirming pregnancy, for routine trimester scans, antenatal checkups, or high-risk maternal care.

👶
Infant & Child Health

Consult pediatrics for milestone developmental evaluations, newborn jaundice, childhood vaccinations, or persistent fever.

🧪
Diagnostic Screenings

Annual health audits, lipid profiling, AMH checks, and preventative cancer screenings recommended based on age and clinical history.

🚑
Urgent & Emergency

For acute abdominal pain, obstetric bleeding, severe breathing distress, or high pediatric fever, visit our 24/7 emergency unit.
