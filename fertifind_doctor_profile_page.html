About Dr. Sara Ahmed

Dr. Sara Ahmed is a distinguished Consultant Fertility Specialist focused on reproductive medicine, comprehensive infertility evaluation, and evidence-based assisted reproductive care. With over 15 years of dedicated practice across leading healthcare facilities in the UAE and Europe, Dr. Ahmed is renowned for her personalized stimulation protocols, empathetic approach, and dedication to answering questions patients commonly ask before choosing a specialist.

She leads clinical protocols at Nova Fertility & IVF Centre in Jumeirah, Dubai, collaborating closely with accredited embryology laboratories to provide patients with transparent guidance on IVF, ICSI, egg vitrification, and reproductive endocrinology.
