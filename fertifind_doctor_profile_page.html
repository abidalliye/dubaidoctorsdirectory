About Dr. Partha Sarathi Das

Dr. Partha Sarathi Das is a distinguished Consultant in Reproductive Medicine and Infertility in Dubai, with over two decades of specialized clinical experience. He currently leads advanced fertility treatments at Orchid Fertility Clinic in Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC) with admitting hospital privileges at American Hospital Dubai.

Having trained at prestigious international reproductive medicine institutions, Dr. Das has helped thousands of couples achieve parenthood through individualized ovarian stimulation, tailored blastocyst culture strategies, and immunological treatment for recurrent implantation failure.

Clinical Expertise & Treatment Scope

Dr. Das provides specialized diagnostic investigations and tailored reproductive protocols:

🧬
Custom IVF & ICSI Protocols

Antagonist, micro-dose flare, and mild-stimulation IVF customized to patient anti-Müllerian hormone (AMH) and antral follicle counts.

🔬
Recurrent Implantation Failure

Endometrial receptivity analysis (ERA), immune factor testing, and personalized embryo transfer timing.

❄️
Fertility Preservation

Oocyte vitrification (social egg freezing) and medical preservation before oncological therapy.

⚕️
Male Factor Infertility

Coordination with reproductive urologists for sperm DNA fragmentation, PESA, and Micro-TESE surgical retrieval.
