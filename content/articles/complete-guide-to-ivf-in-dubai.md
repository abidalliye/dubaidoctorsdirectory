# The Complete Guide to IVF & Fertility Clinics in Dubai

Dubai has emerged as one of the world's leading medical tourism destinations for advanced reproductive medicine and IVF treatments. Regulated strictly under the **Dubai Health Authority (DHA)** and Federal Law No. 7 concerning Human Reproduction, fertility centres across the Emirate offer world-class laboratory standards, high blastocyst culture success rates, and compassionate patient care.

## Key Regulations & DHA Standards

All reproductive endocrinologists and embryology laboratories operating in Dubai must hold current DHA accreditations. Key legal highlights include:

- **Genetic Screening (PGT-A/PGT-M)**: Permitted for family balancing and screening against inherited genetic conditions under registered clinical protocols.
- **Egg & Embryo Cryopreservation**: Permitted for up to 5 years (extendable) with standard annual maintenance agreements.
- **Surrogacy & Donor Gametes**: Strictly governed by UAE federal regulations; couples should verify current eligibility with their licensed reproductive consultant.

## Selecting the Right Clinic

When choosing your fertility specialist on the **Doctors Directory Dubai**, look for:
1. DHA License Verification badge.
2. Verified patient reviews and transparent consultation fees.
3. In-house CAP or ESHRE accredited embryology lab facilities.
